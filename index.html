Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2025

Overview

This Google Sheets Apps Script runs within your Google account. We don’t send your data to external servers.

Data We Access

  • Spreadsheet content and metadata in this file.
  • Protection settings (to copy protections to new sheets).
  • Authorization context required by Google Apps Script.

How We Use Data

  • Duplicate template sheets, set values, update indexes.
  • Copy range/sheet protections.
  • Render a summary dialog or web view and activate target sheets.

What We Don’t Do

  • No selling or sharing data with third parties.
  • No advertising or analytics.
  • No transfer of data to non-Google services.

Temporary Caching

We may use Google’s CacheService (short-lived, within your document) to improve load time.

Security & Access

Access is controlled by your Google Drive/Sheets sharing. Web Apps run “as” the configured account.

Your Choices

  • Disable/remove the script anytime.
  • Delete triggers in Apps Script.
  • Adjust sheet protections and sharing.
